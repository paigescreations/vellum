How it works

1Tap Image to load your reference photo or sketch from your camera roll.
2Drag with one finger to move it, or pinch with two fingers to resize and rotate it against the wall.
3Once it's lined up, tap Lock so it can't shift while you trace. Tap it again to unlock and reposition.
4Adjust has fine opacity and rotation sliders, a mirror flip, a grid, and a camera switch.
