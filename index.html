How it works

1Tap Image to load your reference photo or sketch from your camera roll.
2Drag with one finger to move it, or pinch with two fingers to resize and rotate it against the wall.
3Once it's lined up, tap Lock so it can't shift while you trace. Tap it again to unlock and reposition.
4While locked, swipe one finger left for a stronger image, right to fade it out. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on the view (image and camera together); tap the zoom badge to zoom back out.
5Adjust has fine opacity and rotation sliders, a mirror flip, a grid, a camera switch, and a level.
✦Fit to canvas: in Adjust, tap Fit to canvas. Vellum finds your canvas, puts the four dots on its corners, and tells you which way to tilt or turn the camera until it's square. Tap Fix it for me and the image bends to match the canvas exactly, even if the camera isn't perfectly square. If it can't find the edges, drag the dots onto the corners yourself. You can still move, resize, and turn the image afterward. The fit is remembered until you remove it.
✦Level: the bubble shows sideways tilt; the line below says whether the camera aims too high or low. Canvas leaning back on an easel? Hold the phone flat against it and tap Match canvas angle. With two phones, the viewer shows the tripod's level.
✦Bump alerts: while locked, Vellum watches for the camera getting knocked. If it moves, you get a red warning (plus a buzz and a chime on phones). Tap Re-snap to canvas to line your fit back up without starting over. Turn it off in Adjust.
6Two phones: on the tripod phone, tap This is the tripod camera. Then tap This is my viewer on your other device and scan the code. It connects automatically. Each device just needs internet, from any Wi-Fi or mobile data, and they don't have to be on the same network.
7Your image lives on the viewer. You can load it there with Image, or tap Send image on the tripod to send one across — the tripod never shows it. If the connection drops, tap Reconnect on both and scan again; your image and its position stay put.
