How it works

1Tap Image to load your reference photo or sketch from your camera roll.
2Drag with one finger to move it, or pinch with two fingers to resize and rotate it against the wall.
3Once it's lined up, tap Lock so it can't shift while you trace. Tap it again to unlock and reposition.
4While locked, swipe one finger left for a stronger image, right to fade it out. Pinch with two fingers to zoom in on the view (image and camera together); tap the zoom badge to zoom back out.
5Adjust has fine opacity and rotation sliders, a mirror flip, a grid, and a camera switch.
6Two phones: on the tripod phone, tap This is the tripod camera. Scan its code with your other phone, then hold that phone's reply code up to the tripod phone's back camera. No Wi-Fi network needed — turn on one phone's hotspot and join it from the other.
7Your image lives on the viewer. You can load it there with Image, or tap Send image on the tripod to send one across — the tripod never shows it. If the connection drops, tap Reconnect on both; your image and its position stay put.
